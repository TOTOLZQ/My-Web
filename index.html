About Me

I'm a maker and developer passionate about embedded systems and MicroPython. I love turning small ideas into working hardware projects, from simple button interactions to animated screen displays.

My main playground is the M5Stack Cardputer 1.1 — a compact, hacker-friendly device with a keyboard, screen, and plenty of GPIO. I iterate on each feature in versions, documenting every step along the way.

I believe in open source and share everything under GPL-3.0. Whether you're a beginner or a fellow hardware enthusiast, feel free to explore, fork, and collaborate.
